- The cycle's own retry-band crossed the 10→11 UTC retry-band boundary (non-retry→retry), but PATCH-3c doesn't touch the cycle's own retry-band — the runtime ternary handles it at runtime.

**Why this matters**: A naive reading of "PATCH-3c fetcher-time flip" might assume the retry-band flip should track the cycle's own retry-band. It does NOT — the retry-band flip applies to the FETCHER's retry-band, which is determined by the fetcher's CST time. When the fetcher's CST time crosses a 3-hour retry-band boundary, the retry-band flips even if the cycle's own hour is in a different retry-band direction.

**Rule of thumb**: Always compute the fetcher's UTC hour from the fetcher's CST time, then look up the fetcher's retry-band independently. The cycle's own retry-band is computed from `UTC_HOUR = (NOW_CST.hour - 8) % 24`, which is independent of the fetcher's CST time. The two retry-bands can be in different directions (cycle 892: fetcher retry→non-retry, cycle's own non-retry→retry) and PATCH-3c only describes the fetcher.
